About Kettle Korn Factory

A family tradition with a big-kettle flavor.

We’re a family-owned kettle korn business based in Fruitland, Idaho. Our goal is simple: make the best kettle korn we can, pop it fresh, and share it with people wherever the next great fair or event takes us.
